About Viswanath

Turning complex technology programs into dependable outcomes.

Viswanath is a PMP-certified Senior Delivery Manager, Senior Technical Program Manager, and SAFe 6 Program Consultant with more than 25 years of experience leading digital transformation, enterprise product delivery, and global technology programs.

He has led cross-functional teams of 50+ and governed programs valued up to $5.5M across onshore and offshore delivery. His work includes recovering at-risk programs, improving delivery velocity by up to 40%, and maintaining zero-defect releases through strong quality, financial, risk, and stakeholder governance.

His experience spans banking and financial services, healthcare, automotive, insurance, and technology, including digital banking, global Salesforce platforms, vendor transitions, observability and SRE, DevOps, and legacy modernization.
