About Viswanath

Turning complex technology programs into dependable outcomes.

Viswanath is a PMP-certified IT leader and SAFe Program Consultant with more than 25 years of experience in project management, product management, and technical delivery across global enterprises.

He has led large, complex programs with a focus on stakeholder alignment, financial oversight, risk management, and reliable execution. His approach combines clear governance and metrics with a strong commitment to team development, mentoring, and continuous improvement.

His work spans digital banking, global sales platforms, Salesforce migrations, observability, infrastructure operations, and legacy modernization in banking, healthcare, and other enterprise environments.
