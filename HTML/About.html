About Viswanath

Turning complex technology programs into dependable outcomes.

Viswanath is a PMP-certified IT leader, SAFe 6 Practice Consultant, and Agile Coach with more than 25 years of experience delivering business and technology transformation for global enterprises.

At IBM India, where he has worked since 2005, he has progressed from software engineering into project and program leadership, DevOps consulting, and Agile coaching. He has led cross-functional teams of up to 50 across onshore and offshore locations, delivering complex initiatives from strategy through execution.

His expertise spans program and product management, digital transformation, Agile delivery, cloud adoption, DevOps enablement, financial governance, and risk mitigation. His client work includes digital banking, global Salesforce platforms, observability and SRE, production support, and legacy modernization across banking, healthcare, automotive, insurance, and technology.
